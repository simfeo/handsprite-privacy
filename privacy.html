HandSprite Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

HandSprite is a pixel art editor for Android, published on Google Play by Smbat Makiyan (developer name "unhnar"), Portugal. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. If you have questions, write to xkgsim@gmail.com.

Your drawings stay on your device

Images, projects and palettes you create or open are stored only on your device, or in the location you pick through the Android file picker. HandSprite has no servers, no user accounts and no cloud sync. We never receive your files. The app reads a file only when you open it yourself, through the system picker or by choosing HandSprite from another app.

App settings, such as theme, language, layout and recent files, are saved locally on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of HandSprite shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in Android settings),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and app version,
  • ad interactions, such as impressions and taps,
  • diagnostic data, such as crash information from the SDK.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, HandSprite asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are requested. You can consent, refuse everything, close the form (which counts as a refusal) or choose purpose by purpose. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.

If you are in a US state with a consumer privacy law, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for advertising.

You can change your choice at any time in the app under View > Ad privacy.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

HandSprite offers a one-time purchase, "Remove ads". Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app receives from Google Play only whether the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off, and a purchase token used to confirm it. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. After you buy Remove ads, the app stops loading ads altogether.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and talk to Google Play.
  • Billing: for the Remove ads purchase.
  • Advertising ID: used by the AdMob SDK as described above.

HandSprite does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

Children

HandSprite is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because we do not run servers or store personal data ourselves, there is nothing about you in our hands to access, correct or delete. For data collected by Google for advertising or payments, you can use the controls described above, your Android settings and your Google Account. If you are in the EEA or UK you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
